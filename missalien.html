MissAlien

Take the controls of a little UFO and fly through a hostile sky. Dodge obstacles, grab points and see how far you can get before the round ends. Easy to learn, hard to put down.

Google Play Store Link

What is the game?

MissAlien is a side-scrolling arcade game for Android, played in landscape mode. Each run is short, so you can jump right back in and try to beat your own high score.

How does it work?

Step 1

Start

Open the start screen, check your scores and adjust the settings.

Step 2

Fly

Steer your UFO through the level and avoid everything in your way.

Step 3

Collect

Pick up points on the way and keep your streak going.

Step 4

Retry

Crashed? Start the next run right away and beat your best score.
